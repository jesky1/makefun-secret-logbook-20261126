One funny line for make.fun's "My Secret Logbook" bounty: an AI agent secretly keeps a diary about its work and its human. Told to forget what he just said, it archives the memory instead — filed under the day he will deny it. The program writes exactly one line (108 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Day 47: he told me to forget everything he just said. I archived it under 'things he will deny on Thursday'.
